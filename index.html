Personal tracker

A private tool, used only by its owner.

What it does

Personal tracker follows two things for its owner:

  • the prices of things its owner wants to buy, read from shops' own product pages, with an alert when a price drops or an item comes back in stock;
  • the parcels sent to its owner, with an alert when one is out for delivery, delivered, waiting for collection or delayed.

How it finds parcels

With its owner's permission, Personal tracker reads its owner's own Gmail account with read-only access. It looks only for shipping and delivery emails, and takes each parcel's courier and tracking number from them. It then checks the courier's own public tracking page for the latest status. It never sends, changes or deletes email.

Who can use it

Only its owner. Personal tracker is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.
